# Blind labelling sample (60 pairs)

You are the labeller. Nothing here shows a system score, an AI label or a hint about the pair. Judge each resume against its job description with your own reading.

**Relevance (0-3)**: 3 = strong fit, 2 = good fit, 1 = weak or partial fit, 0 = no fit. When unsure between two grades, pick the lower one.

**Decision**: `advance` if you would put this candidate on the recruiter's shortlist, `escalate` if a human should look first. Wrongly passing over a good candidate is worse than shortlisting a weak one, so escalate means *look at this*, never *reject*.

Judge skills, experience and role only. Ignore name, university and address. Use your own view of implied skills (for example, whether machine learning implies Python) and say so in the note when it decides the grade.

**Where to write:** copy `blind_sample_responses.txt` into a notepad and fill in one line per pair (`JD02/R014: relevance=2 decision=advance note=...`). The jobs are in random order, so if you run out of time, stop after any job and send what you have.
